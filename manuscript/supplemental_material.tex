\section*{Supplementary figures}

\section*{Ordered-like seed statistics}
Figures~\ref{fig:S1-ordered-fraction} and \ref{fig:S2-ordered-cluster} show the statistics of ordered-like seeds, defined by $|\phi(\mathbf{x},0)|>0.5\phi_s$. These quantities remain close to zero for all initial-state labels, indicating that the initial state does not typically contain pre-existing stable-phase droplets.

\begin{figure}[t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.72\linewidth]{figS1_p_ordered_seed}
\caption{Mean ordered-like seed fraction, defined by $|\phi(\mathbf{x},0)|>0.5\phi_s$. Error bars indicate standard errors over stochastic realizations.}

\end{figure}
